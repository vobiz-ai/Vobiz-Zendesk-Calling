Frequently asked

Why does the person I call hear ringing after answering?

Vobiz dials the customer first, then bridges your browser in. Those few seconds of ringback are that second leg connecting. It is expected, and the call order cannot be reversed.

Which ticket does a call get logged to?

The ticket you are viewing when the call ends. If you started the call by clicking a number on a ticket, that ticket is used. With no ticket open, a new one is created.

Do I need a headset?

Audio runs through your computer's microphone and speakers. Your browser asks for microphone permission on the first call — allow it, or the other side hears nothing.

Is my Auth Token stored anywhere?

Not by this app. It is held in your browser session and sent to your own calling backend on each request. Recording links written into tickets carry an expiring token, never your credentials.
